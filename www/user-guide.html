Installing RecoverySky

RecoverySky is a native device app with Zoom build in! To install the app on your device, you may navigate to the Android Play Store and install directly from there. Zoom is integrated directly into RecoverySky and does not require any Zoom specific instructions or authorization.

Usage

Instructions for using the app are as follows

  • Launch the app
  • A list is populated with all the currently live NA meetings
  • You may change your in-meeting name using the Username input field at the top of the list
  • Click on the meeting you wish to join
  • Zoom will automatically launch and join the selected meeting with your Username
  • Zoom user in meeting controls are documented here

Removing the App

RecoverySky does not require any specific de-authorization steps as Zoom is integrated directly into the app.

Removing RecoverySky from your device will delete and and all associated data from your device.

You may remove RecoverySky from your Android device by following standard device operating instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to log into my Zoom account: No
  • Does RecoverySky have access to any of my Zoom account data: No
  • Is my attendance in recovery meetings completely anonymous: Absolutely
  • Will there be an iOS version: Yes
